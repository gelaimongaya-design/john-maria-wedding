12.01.20 - The First Meet

Maria was a new hire, welcomed by the team over dinner in Makati. Somehow, she and John ended at the same table. Just the usual office chaos, everyone teasing them.

Neither was looking for love. They were simply two people sharing a table, completely unaware they’d someday become each other’s favorite person.

08.01.22 - The Love Begins

Between busy seasons and fieldwork, something quietly grew between them. In front of Mt. Mayon, they finally said what they’d been feeling.

From there, love found its rhythm in Metro, through late-night ramen dates and the comfort of simply being together during overtime.

08.01.26 - The “Yes” Day

Four years later, on the same date their love began, they decided to choose each other for a lifetime, this time under the stars while camping in Laguna.

02.02.27 - The Best Day

What began in Bicol, grew through their days in Metro, and became forever in Laguna. Now, with God at the center and the people they love beside them, they’re ready for the next chapter.

Ceremony & Reception

We’ve chosen to have a simple intimate wedding and dinner with our closest family and friends at Timmy in the Woods.

Dress Code

A forest affair calls for formal elegance. Please wear long dresses and suits with ties in black and/or white.

Transportation

The venue is accessible via Grab or private car. The designated parking area is located outside the venue. Please allow at least 2 hours of travel time and plan accordingly, as traffic conditions may cause delays.

Notes from the Woods

*Squirels, peacocks, birds, and other woodland friends may join us.
Please enjoy them from a distance and avoid touching or feeding them.

*Mosquitos may be present, so please come prepared.
